Intermittent Fasting & FTO Satiety Variants

How to optimize time-restricted feeding (16/8 or 14/10) when carrying FTO hunger variants without triggering rebound overeating.

💡 Key Biological Insights

  • FTO A allele carriers produce higher post-meal ghrelin and experience intense hunger spikes during extended fasting windows.
  • Extreme 24-48 hour fasts often lead to compensatory overeating of hyper-palatable energy-dense foods.
  • A 14-hour overnight fast (e.g. 7 PM to 9 AM) combined with a 35g+ protein breakfast stabilizes central satiety pathways effortlessly.

🥗 Practical Dietary Guidelines

  • Break fasts with high-protein, high-fiber meals rather than refined carbohydrates.
  • Maintain consistent daily eating windows to train ghrelin secretion timing.
  • Ensure adequate hydration and electrolyte intake during fasting hours.
