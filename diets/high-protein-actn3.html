💡 Key Biological Insights

  • ACTN3 R/R power genotypes undergo higher muscular mechanical strain and require consistent amino acid availability for myofibrillar repair.
  • X/X endurance genotypes benefit from increased post-exercise collagen peptide and Vitamin C intake to support tendinous adaptation.
  • Distributing protein intake evenly into 30-40g boluses every 3-4 hours optimizes muscle protein synthesis via mTOR signaling.

🥗 Practical Dietary Guidelines

  • Consume 30-40g high-quality protein within 60 minutes post-workout.
  • Supplement with 5g daily creatine monohydrate for ACTN3 R/R explosive phosphocreatine support.
  • Pair post-workout protein with complex carbs to replenish glycogen stores.
