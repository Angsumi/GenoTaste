💡 Key Biological Insights

  • Synthetic folic acid found in fortified white flour and low-quality supplements competes with natural folates for transport proteins.
  • Natural folates from spinach, romaine, avocado, and liver provide bioavailable tetrahydrofolates naturally.
  • Choline (egg yolks, liver) and betaine (beets, spinach) supply parallel methyl-donor pathways via BHMT enzyme.

🥗 Practical Dietary Guidelines

  • Eliminate foods fortified with synthetic folic acid.
  • Include 2 eggs daily or egg yolks for essential choline methyl-donor support.
  • Incorporate cooked beets, spinach, and dark leafy greens daily.
