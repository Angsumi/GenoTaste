💡 Key Biological Insights

  • PPARG Ala allele carriers experience optimal insulin sensitivity and weight management when consuming oleic-acid-rich monounsaturated fats.
  • Polyphenol-rich extra virgin olive oil acts as a natural signaling molecule for anti-inflammatory transcription factors.
  • Fiber-dense legumes and intact whole grains provide sustained glycemic response.

🥗 Practical Dietary Guidelines

  • Use extra virgin olive oil as the primary added fat source for all meals.
  • Include at least 3 servings of wild oily fish (sardines, mackerel, salmon) per week.
  • Incorporate abundant legumes (lentils, chickpeas) and colorful Mediterranean vegetables.
