📖 Understanding Coffee Clearance & Sleep

Caffeine clearance is governed primarily by your liver's CYP1A2 enzyme speed and your brain's ADORA2A adenosine receptors:

  • Fast Coffee Clearers (CYP1A2 A/A): Process 50% of ingested caffeine in 2 to 4 hours. Morning or early afternoon coffee leaves minimal residual caffeine by bedtime.
  • Slow Coffee Clearers (CYP1A2 A/C or C/C): Retain active caffeine in blood plasma for 8 to 10+ hours. A 2:00 PM coffee can linger into bedtime hours.
  • Deep Sleep Impact: Even if you fall asleep easily, carrying >20mg of residual caffeine at bedtime blocks adenosine sleep pressure receptors, suppressing Stage N3 slow-wave deep sleep.
