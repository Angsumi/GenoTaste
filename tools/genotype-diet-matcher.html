📖 Why Universal Diets Fail for Individual Biology

Different bodies process fats, proteins, and carbohydrates differently based on key genomic markers:

  • APOE Saturated Fat Response: APOE4 carriers down-regulate cholesterol clearance on high-saturated-fat keto diets (butter, bacon), requiring extra virgin olive oil and omega-3s instead.
  • FTO Appetite & Hunger Hormones: FTO A carriers experience higher post-meal ghrelin hunger spikes, requiring higher protein (30g+ per meal) and fiber to stay full.
  • PPARG Insulin Sensitivity: Governs how efficiently cells clear glucose and process monounsaturated fats.
  • ACTN3 Muscle Fiber Type: Sprint power (R/R) vs endurance (X/X) dictates protein pacing and workout recovery.
