MTHFR: B-Vitamin & Energy Methylation (MTHFR Gene)

Helps your body convert dietary folate into bioactive L-methylfolate—essential for daily energy production, brain focus, mood, and detoxification.

🧬 Biological Mechanism & Pathways

The MTHFR gene provides instructions for making an enzyme that converts folate into its active coenzyme form (5-MTHF). This active form donates methyl groups to clear homocysteine and build neurotransmitters (dopamine, serotonin). Variants (C677T and A1298C) reduce enzyme efficiency by 30% to 70%, requiring natural bioactive folates rather than synthetic folic acid.

📊 Genotype Variants & Health Impact

Normal Folate Conversion (677 C/C) ~45% of population

100% enzymatic conversion of dietary folates.

GenoTaste Recommendation: Maintain balanced intake of leafy green vegetables.
Mild Reduction (677 C/T) ~40% of population

~65% enzymatic efficiency. Mild reduction in methylation capacity.

GenoTaste Recommendation: Choose natural folates (spinach, asparagus, lentils) over synthetic folic acid.
Significant Reduction (677 T/T) ~10-15% of population

~30% enzymatic efficiency. Requires active L-methylfolate to support optimal energy.

GenoTaste Recommendation: Incorporate bioavailable L-methylfolate (5-MTHF) and active B12 under consulting guidance.

🥗 Key Dietary Adjustments

  • Eat abundant dark leafy greens (spinach, romaine, avocado, lentils) rich in natural folates.
  • Avoid synthetic folic acid in fortified processed foods, as un-metabolized folic acid can block cell receptors.
  • Ensure adequate Vitamin B2 (riboflavin) intake, an essential helper molecule for MTHFR enzyme stability.

⚡ High-Leverage Lifestyle Actions

  • Test plasma homocysteine levels annually as part of routine preventive wellness.
  • Support liver detoxification pathways with natural choline (egg yolks) and betaine.
  • Consult GenoTaste specialists to evaluate your bioavailable B-complex balance.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Folic Acid and Methylfolate?

Folic acid is a synthetic form of folate that requires multiple liver conversions (including MTHFR). L-Methylfolate (5-MTHF) is already active and ready for your body to use immediately.

Does carrying an MTHFR variant mean I have a health condition?

No! MTHFR variants are common biological traits, not diseases. They simply indicate a nutritional preference for bioactive folates and natural whole foods.
