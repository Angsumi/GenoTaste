FUT2: Fucosyltransferase 2 (Gut Secretor & B12 Absorption Gene)

FUT2 determines secretor status—whether blood group antigens are secreted into the gut mucosa, directly shaping microbiome diversity and Vitamin B12 transport.

🧬 Biological Mechanism & Pathways

FUT2 encodes an alpha-1,2-fucosyltransferase that synthesizes the H antigen in mucosal secretions. Secretors (FUT2 positive) provide fucosylated glycans that nourish beneficial Bifidobacteria strains in the gut lining. Non-secretors (rs601338 G/G) lack mucosal antigens, resulting in altered gut flora composition, lower intrinsic Bifidobacteria, and reduced intrinsic B12 absorption efficiency.

📊 Genotype Variants & Health Impact

Secretor (FUT2 Active) ~80% of population

Abundant mucosal glycans; healthy Bifidobacteria colonization and standard B12 intestinal transport.

GenoTaste Recommendation: Maintain prebiotic fiber intake to feed indigenous beneficial gut strains.
Non-Secretor (FUT2 Inactive rs601338 G/G) ~20% of population

Absence of ABO blood group antigens in saliva and gut secretions; ~30% lower baseline Bifidobacteria and reduced B12 absorption capacity.

GenoTaste Recommendation: Supplement with targeted Bifidobacterium probiotics and check serum Vitamin B12 / Methylmalonic acid (MMA).

🥗 Key Dietary Adjustments

  • Non-secretors should prioritize fermented foods (kefir, sauerkraut, kimchi) to supply live beneficial bacteria.
  • Consume prebiotic galactooligosaccharides (GOS) and acacia fiber to support microbiome diversity.
  • Ensure bioavailable B12 (methylcobalamin/adenosylcobalamin) intake if serum levels drop below optimal ranges.

⚡ High-Leverage Lifestyle Actions

  • Perform a GI-MAP or gut microbiome test if digestive irregularity occurs.
  • Avoid overuse of non-essential NSAIDs or antibiotics that disrupt the gut mucosal barrier.
  • Pair gut optimization with MTHFR methylation management for complete cellular energy balance.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a non-secretor?

A non-secretor lacks functional FUT2 enzyme activity, meaning their body does not secrete blood group antigens into body fluids like saliva or gut mucus. This shapes their intestinal ecosystem.

How does FUT2 relate to B12 deficiency?

Non-secretors often have lower expression of intrinsic factor co-receptors in the ileum, leading to reduced efficiency in absorbing dietary B12.
