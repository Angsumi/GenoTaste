Evidence-based biological explanations, variant impacts, and personalized nutrition strategies for key health and performance genes.

Sleep & Rhythms
CYP1A2
Cytochrome P450 1A2 (Caffeine Metabolism Gene)

CYP1A2 encodes the primary liver enzyme responsible for 95% of caffeine clearance. Variations determine whether you are a fast or slow caffeine metabolizer, directly impacting sleep architecture, cardiovascular stress, and exercise endurance timing.

Fatigue & Energy
MTHFR
Methylenetetrahydrofolate Reductase (B-Vitamin Methylation Gene)

MTHFR converts dietary folate into its active coenzyme form (L-methylfolate), critical for cellular methylation, DNA repair, neurotransmitter synthesis, and homocysteine conversion.

Body & Weight
APOE
Apolipoprotein E (Lipid Transport & Saturated Fat Gene)

APOE plays a primary role in cholesterol transport, lipid clearance, and neuro-vascular maintenance. Variant alleles (E2, E3, E4) dictate how your body responds to dietary saturated fats and cholesterol.

Fitness & Recovery
ACTN3
Alpha-Actinin-3 (Sprint vs Endurance Muscle Gene)

ACTN3 is known as the 'gene for speed'. It controls the expression of alpha-actinin-3 protein in fast-twitch glycolytic muscle fibers, influencing explosive power, strength output, and muscle damage recovery rates.

FTO
Fat Mass and Obesity-Associated Gene (Satiety & Appetite)

FTO influences energy homeostasis, appetite regulation, and central ghrelin sensitivity. Variants alter post-meal satiety signals and snacking impulses.

COMT breaks down catecholamines (dopamine, epinephrine, norepinephrine) in the prefrontal cortex. Val158Met variants dictate your cognitive focus style ('Warrior' vs 'Explorer').
