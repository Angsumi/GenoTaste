Lactose Persistence & Gut Microbiome Balance

Differentiate between genetic lactase non-persistence (LCT C/C) and milk protein sensitivities, and learn how FUT2 shapes beneficial gut flora.

💡 Core Key Takeaways

  • LCT C/C genotype leads to natural adult down-regulation of small intestine lactase enzymes.
  • Fermented dairy (kefir, aged cheeses) contains bacterial lactase, rendering it highly digestible.
  • FUT2 non-secretors have lower baseline Bifidobacteria and require targeted probiotic support.

🧬 Biological Mechanism & Pathways

Lactase breakdown occurs at the intestinal brush border. Uncleaved lactose passes into the colon, drawing water osmotic ally and fermenting into gas. FUT2 secretor status determines mucosal glycan availability for beneficial commensal bacteria.

🎯 Actionable Action Steps

  • Choose naturally low-lactose aged cheeses (parmesan, cheddar) or fermented kefir if LCT C/C.
  • Supplement with Bifidobacterium probiotic strains if FUT2 non-secretor.
  • Use plant-based milks fortified with calcium and Vitamin D3/K2.
