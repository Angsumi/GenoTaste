Sleep & Rhythms

Adenosine Dynamics & Circadian Sleep Pacing

How COMT neurotransmitter clearing and ADORA2A adenosine receptor sensitivity shape evening sleep architecture and morning wakefulness.

💡 Core Key Takeaways

  • COMT Met/Met carriers retain higher evening dopamine and require active wind-down routines.
  • ADORA2A T/T carriers experience reduced Stage N3 deep sleep if stimulant intake occurs after mid-day.
  • Aligning light exposure and nutrient timing with genetic rhythm profiles optimizes restorative sleep.

🧬 Biological Mechanism & Pathways

Sleep pressure builds through adenosine accumulation in the brain. Simultaneously, catecholamines are cleared by COMT. High adenosine receptor sensitivity paired with slow dopamine clearing creates sleep onset friction if caffeine or evening stress is present.

🎯 Actionable Action Steps

  • Get 10-15 minutes of bright morning sunlight to anchor central circadian clock oscillations.
  • Incorporate evening magnesium bisglycinate and L-theanine.
  • Set strict digital blue-light boundaries 90 minutes before bedtime.
